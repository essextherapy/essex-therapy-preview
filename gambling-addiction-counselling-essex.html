Addiction counselling

Gambling Addiction Counselling in Essex

Gambling can quietly take over life, finances and relationships. Support is available, and it is never too late to ask for it.

Contact Me

Understanding

How can counselling help with gambling?

Counselling helps you understand what gambling gives you, whether excitement, escape or a way to cope, and what it is costing. Together we work on regaining control and repairing relationships.

Partners and families affected by gambling are welcome to seek support too.

You may recognise

  • Gambling more than planned
  • Chasing losses
  • Lying or hiding gambling
  • Money worries or debt
  • Restlessness when not gambling
  • Strain on relationships and work
