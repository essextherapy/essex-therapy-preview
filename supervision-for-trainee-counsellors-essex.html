Understanding

What is supervision for trainee counsellors?

Supervision is a regular, confidential space to discuss your client work, explore ethical questions and develop your skills. For trainees on placement, it is essential for safe, reflective practice.

I offer a supportive and collaborative approach, with an emphasis on your growth.

You may recognise

  • Unsure how to handle a case
  • Worry about making mistakes
  • Managing client risk
  • Feeling overwhelmed by the work
  • Balancing placement and training
  • Developing your style
