Understanding

What is group supervision?

Group supervision brings practitioners together to reflect on their work with a facilitator. It offers diverse perspectives, shared learning and less isolation.

It can be offered to teams, services or independent practitioners.

You may recognise

  • Feeling isolated in practice
  • Wanting peer learning
  • Limited supervision budget
  • Services needing regular supervision
  • Wanting varied perspectives
  • Developing reflective practice
