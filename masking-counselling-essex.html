Understanding

What is masking?

Masking is hiding or suppressing parts of yourself, such as natural reactions, interests or needs, to fit in or stay safe. It is common among autistic and ADHD people and others who learned they needed to hide.

Over time masking can lead to exhaustion, anxiety and loss of identity.

You may recognise

  • Exhaustion after being around people
  • Rehearsing conversations
  • Not knowing who you are underneath
  • Hiding stims, interests or needs
  • Burnout or shutdown
  • Fear of being found out

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is masking always bad?

Not always, but constant masking is costly. We can explore when it helps and when it harms.

Can I stop masking altogether?

Not necessarily. It is about choice and safety.
