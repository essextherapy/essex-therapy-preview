Supporting Families Affected by Addiction in Essex

Living with someone else’s addiction is exhausting and often lonely. You deserve support in your own right.

Contact Me

Understanding

How does addiction affect families?

Addiction affects the whole family, bringing worry, secrecy, conflict, broken trust and changing roles. Partners, parents, siblings and children can all be deeply affected.

Counselling gives you space to look after yourself, set boundaries and decide what you need.

You may recognise

  • Constant worry or being on high alert
  • Feeling responsible for their behaviour
  • Walking on eggshells
  • Anger, guilt or resentment
  • Loss of trust
  • Neglecting your own needs
