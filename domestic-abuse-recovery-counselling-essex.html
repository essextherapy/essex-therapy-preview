Domestic Abuse Recovery Counselling in Essex

Recovering from domestic abuse takes time and courage. I offer a confidential, trauma-informed space that moves at your pace.

Contact Me

Understanding

What is domestic abuse recovery counselling?

Counselling after domestic abuse supports you to process what happened, recover your sense of safety and rebuild confidence and trust. It can help whether you are still in the relationship, have left or left long ago.

If you are in immediate danger, please call 999. Specialist services can also help with safety planning.

You may recognise

  • Fear, anxiety or hypervigilance
  • Guilt or self-blame
  • Difficulty trusting new people
  • Loss of confidence and independence
  • Flashbacks or nightmares
  • Concern about the effect on children

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is it confidential?

Yes, within the usual limits of counselling confidentiality, which we go through at the start.

Can I come while still in the relationship?

Yes. We can talk about what feels safe, including how you contact me.
