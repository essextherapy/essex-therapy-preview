Affair Recovery Counselling in Essex

Discovering an affair can be devastating. Counselling helps you both to process what happened and decide how to move forward.

Contact Me

Understanding

How does counselling help after an affair?

After an affair, emotions can swing between anger, grief and confusion. Couples counselling offers a balanced space to talk honestly, understand what led to it and decide whether and how to rebuild trust.

You can also come on your own, whether you want to stay or leave.

You may recognise

  • Shock, anger or deep hurt
  • Obsessive thoughts or checking
  • Guilt and shame
  • Loss of trust
  • Uncertainty about staying or going
  • Difficulty talking without it escalating
