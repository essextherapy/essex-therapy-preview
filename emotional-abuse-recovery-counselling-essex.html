Understanding

What is emotional abuse?

Emotional abuse includes criticism, control, put-downs, manipulation, isolation and making you doubt your own perceptions. It can happen in couples, families and workplaces.

Because it is often subtle, many people struggle to name it. Counselling helps you make sense of what happened and rebuild trust in yourself.

You may recognise

  • Constantly doubting yourself
  • Walking on eggshells
  • Feeling responsible for others’ moods
  • Loss of confidence or identity
  • Isolation from friends and family
  • Confusion about what was real

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if it was emotional abuse?

If you feel diminished, controlled or afraid, it is worth exploring. We can talk it through safely.

Can I come after the relationship has ended?

Yes, many people do. Recovery often happens after leaving.
