Childhood Trauma Counselling in Essex

Difficult experiences in childhood can shape how we feel, trust and relate long into adulthood. Counselling offers a safe space to make sense of them.

Contact Me

Understanding

How does childhood trauma affect adults?

Childhood trauma, including neglect, abuse, loss or growing up in a frightening or unpredictable home, can affect our confidence, emotions and relationships as adults. Many people only link their struggles to childhood much later.

Counselling helps you understand those links and develop new ways of coping, without needing to relive everything at once.

You may recognise

  • Difficulty trusting or feeling safe
  • Choosing unhealthy relationships
  • Low self-worth or harsh self-criticism
  • Anxiety, anger or emotional numbness
  • People-pleasing or perfectionism
  • Feeling stuck in old patterns
