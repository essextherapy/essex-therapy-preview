Understanding

What are compassion fatigue and secondary trauma?

Compassion fatigue is emotional exhaustion from caring for others. Secondary trauma can develop from repeated exposure to other people’s traumatic experiences. Both are common in teaching, healthcare, social care, policing and counselling.

Counselling gives you a place to process what you carry and protect your wellbeing.

You may recognise

  • Feeling numb or drained
  • Intrusive thoughts about cases
  • Irritability or hopelessness
  • Difficulty switching off
  • Avoiding work or people
  • Losing joy in the job you love
