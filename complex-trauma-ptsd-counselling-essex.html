Understanding

What is complex trauma?

Complex trauma usually develops after repeated or long-lasting experiences, often in childhood or in relationships where escape was difficult. Post-traumatic stress can follow a single overwhelming event or ongoing exposure.

Counselling focuses first on safety and stability, then on understanding your reactions and gently working through what has happened.

You may recognise

  • Flashbacks, nightmares or intrusive memories
  • Always feeling on guard
  • Emotional numbness or sudden emotional storms
  • Avoiding reminders
  • Difficulty with trust and closeness
  • Shame and a damaged sense of self

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is PTSD different from complex trauma?

They overlap. PTSD often follows a specific event, while complex trauma follows repeated experiences. Counselling can help with both.

Do I need a diagnosis to come?

No. You do not need a diagnosis to get support.
