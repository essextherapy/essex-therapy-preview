Understanding

What is burnout?

Burnout is a state of deep physical and emotional exhaustion caused by prolonged stress, often at work or in caring roles. It can bring cynicism, reduced performance and a sense of emptiness.

Counselling helps you understand what is driving it, what you need and how to recover sustainably.

You may recognise

  • Constant exhaustion
  • Cynicism or detachment
  • Dread about work
  • Poor sleep or concentration
  • Irritability or tearfulness
  • Feeling ineffective

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if I have burnout?

If you are exhausted, detached and struggling to cope for a long time, it is worth speaking to someone.

Do I need time off work?

That depends on your situation. If you are very unwell, speak to your GP.
