Understanding

What is clinical supervision?

Clinical supervision is a regular, confidential space to reflect on your client work, explore ethical questions, manage risk and keep developing as a practitioner. It protects clients and supports you.

I offer supervision that is supportive, reflective, collaborative and focused on your development.

Who I supervise

  • Qualified counsellors
  • Trainee counsellors
  • Charities and community services
  • Private practitioners
  • Organisations and teams
  • Helping professionals working with trauma, addiction, children and young people

What we explore

What happens in supervision

Supervision is a protected space to think about your work: your clients, your decisions and how the work is affecting you.

I offer a supportive and reflective approach, with a clear focus on ethical, safe practice.

  • Risk and safeguarding
  • Ethics and professional boundaries
  • Transference and countertransference
  • Burnout and self-care
  • Complex cases
  • Working with trauma and addiction
  • Supporting neurodiverse clients
  • Your professional development
