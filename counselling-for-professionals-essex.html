Understanding

Who is this for?

I work with people in demanding roles, including teachers, police and emergency services, NHS and social care staff, prison staff, managers, executives and fellow counsellors.

With my own background in public-sector and complex settings, I understand the pressures of responsibility, risk and constant demand.

Who I work with

Professionals in demanding roles

People who care for others, lead teams or carry heavy responsibility are often the least likely to ask for help for themselves.

I understand the pressure of public-sector, high-risk and caring roles, and I offer a private space just for you.

Why people wait

Why professionals delay seeking help

There are many understandable reasons people put off getting support.

Fear

Worry about what it might say about you or your role.

Stigma

Concern about how colleagues or employers might see you.

Responsibility

Feeling you have to be the strong one for everyone else.

Perfectionism

Believing you should be able to cope on your own.

Isolation

Not knowing who is safe to talk to.

Time

Believing there is never a good moment to stop.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will my employer know?

No. Sessions are confidential and private, within the usual limits we discuss at the start.

I'm the one who supports others. Is it okay to need help?

Yes. Looking after your own wellbeing helps you sustain the work.

Can we meet online or outside working hours?

Please get in touch to discuss what is available.

Do you offer support for workplace trauma?

Yes. We can work with the effects of difficult incidents and ongoing pressure.

Is counselling for professionals confidential?

Yes. Sessions are private, within the usual limits of counselling confidentiality, which we go through at the start.

Can you help with burnout?

Yes. We can look at what is driving it, what you need, and how to recover sustainably.
