Understanding

What are complex family relationships?

Complex family relationships involve long-standing conflict, hurt or distance between parents, adult children, siblings, step-families or grandparents. They may include estrangement, addiction, mental illness, neglect or abuse.

Childhood experiences often continue into adulthood, shaping attachment, family roles and the patterns we repeat. Counselling helps you understand them and choose differently.

Common difficulties

  • Conflict, estrangement or distance
  • Setting boundaries without guilt
  • Parentification and being the family caretaker
  • Growing up with addiction or mental illness
  • Emotional neglect
  • Difficult step-family or sibling relationships
  • People-pleasing and perfectionism
  • Deciding how much contact feels right

Family difficulties

When family is complicated

Family relationships can hold deep love and deep hurt at the same time. Whatever your situation, you are not alone.

Counselling offers a place to think things through without pressure or judgement.

  • Conflict and long-standing tension
  • Estrangement from parents or siblings
  • Parentification and being the caretaker
  • Growing up with addiction or mental illness
  • Emotional neglect or abuse
  • Step-family and blended-family strain
  • Parenting concerns
  • Grief within the family

Patterns

Patterns that can follow us into adulthood

Early family roles often continue into adult life, quietly shaping our relationships, work and self-esteem.

People-pleasing

Putting everyone else first and struggling to say no.

Caretaking

Feeling responsible for others' feelings and problems.

Avoidance

Keeping your distance to stay safe from conflict or hurt.

Hyper-independence

Struggling to ask for help or rely on anyone.

Perfectionism

Never feeling good enough, however much you do.

Family roles

Being the scapegoat, the peacemaker or the strong one.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Should I cut contact with a family member?

That is your decision. Counselling gives you space to think it through safely, without pressure either way.

Can siblings repair their relationship?

Sometimes, yes. It depends on both people. We can explore what repair might look like.

How do I stop feeling guilty?

Guilt often reflects old family rules. We can look at where it comes from and what is really yours to carry.

Do you see families together?

Yes, where it is appropriate. We can discuss what would work best.

How do I cope with a difficult or narcissistic parent?

Every situation is different. Counselling can help you understand the dynamics, set boundaries and protect your wellbeing.

Can I come on my own about my family?

Yes. Many people come alone, and that is often where change starts.
