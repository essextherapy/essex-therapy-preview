Couples Counselling in Essex

Whether you are facing a crisis or a slow drift apart, couples counselling offers a balanced, non-judgemental space to understand each other and rebuild trust, communication and closeness.

Understanding

What happens in couples counselling?

Couples counselling gives both partners a safe, balanced place to be heard. We look at the patterns that keep repeating, what is really being said beneath the arguments, and how you each respond under stress.

The aim is not to decide who is right, but to help you understand one another and decide together what you want.

Why couples seek counselling

  • Communication has broken down
  • Frequent arguments or distance
  • Trust after an affair
  • Differences over parenting or blended family life
  • Intimacy difficulties
  • The impact of trauma, addiction or loss
  • Life transitions and change
  • Deciding whether to stay or separate

Common difficulties

Why couples come to counselling

Most couples do not arrive because of one big thing. More often it is a build-up of unspoken hurt, distance or repeated arguments.

Coming for help is a sign you want things to change.

Attachment

Understanding attachment in relationships

The way we learned to connect early in life shapes how we love, argue and protect ourselves as adults. Understanding your patterns, and your partner's, can change everything.

Secure

Comfortable with closeness and independence, and able to trust.

Avoidant

Tends to pull away and rely on self when things feel close or tense.

Anxious

Tends to seek reassurance and fear being left or not good enough.

Disorganised

Wanting closeness and fearing it at the same time, often after early hurt.

Patterns can change

Understanding your style is the first step to relating differently.

Both of you matter

Each partner has a voice in a balanced, fair space.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Should we come before separation?

Yes, coming earlier often helps. It can also help if you are unsure whether to stay together.

Can one partner attend first?

Yes. Sometimes one person makes the first contact. We can talk about how to involve your partner.

How many sessions will we need?

It depends on your goals. We review together regularly.

Is it confidential?

Yes, within the usual limits of counselling confidentiality, which we go through at the start.

How do we know if couples counselling is right for us?

If you feel stuck, distant or are arguing more, it is worth a conversation. We can talk about whether it is the right time.

What if my partner does not want to come?

You can attend alone first. Many people find that changes things.
